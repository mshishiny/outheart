The Sanctuary

Outheart

In a culture of closed doors and high walls, women often carry their truths in silence. Outheart was born as a sanctuary — a safe space where hearts can open fully, free from judgment, fear, or taboo. Here, we honor the feminine in all her expressions: her voice, her needs, her desires, her vulnerabilities, and her strength.

Outheart is not just a space — it is a movement of trust, healing, and empowerment. Through circles of connection, shared wisdom, and creative expression, we nurture a community where women can reclaim their power, embrace their wholeness, and step into the world unapologetically.

"At Outheart, the walls fall away, and the heart leads the way."

How we gather

Circles & offerings

However you arrive, you're received exactly as you are. Three ways to move through Outheart together.

I.

Workshops

Focused sessions on a single thread — voice, boundaries, creativity, rest — held with intention and room to feel.

II.

Healing circles

Ongoing circles where women return, again and again, to be witnessed and to witness one another.

III.

Retreats

Extended time away together, in safe venues chosen with care, to rest fully into the body and the group.
